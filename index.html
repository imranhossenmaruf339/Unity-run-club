Km 02 — Our Mission

Why We Run

  • To inspire people to begin their running journey
  • To organize meaningful, well-planned running events
  • To create a supportive community for beginners and athletes
  • To promote fitness awareness in schools, universities and workplaces
  • To contribute to a healthier society through movement and education
Founders' Message

Unity Run Club was born from a simple belief — that running can change lives. It strengthens the body, builds confidence, and develops discipline while bringing people together under one positive spirit.

We are at the beginning of our journey. Although we have not yet organized major events, we carry a powerful dream: to make running a popular and life-changing movement across Bangladesh — a place where new runners find encouragement, young people grow passionate about fitness, and individuals embrace healthier lifestyles.

Our slogan, "Run For Bangladesh," is more than words — it is a commitment to build a healthier nation and a stronger generation.

— Team Unity Run Club
